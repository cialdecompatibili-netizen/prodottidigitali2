A ready-made digital product saves time only if it fits what you need to do. Here is a short guide to choosing.

## Start from the problem

Before looking at the catalogue, write in one line what you want to achieve: a website to put online, a sheet to keep the accounts, a text to reuse. If the product solves exactly that problem, the rest is detail.

## Check what is included

Read the product page carefully: file format, programs needed, instructions and what you can change. A good product states clearly what you receive.

## Adapt it, don't use it as it is

Change names, colours, texts and numbers to your own. A template works better when it looks made for you, and a few tweaks are usually enough.
